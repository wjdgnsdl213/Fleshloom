# M1 First-time Play-test Sheet

M1 is not complete until five people who did not design the mechanic try it. An AI reviewer can find code defects, but it cannot replace first-time hand feel.

## Setup

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:5173` from the `fleshloom@0.3.0` development build.
- Use the same laptop/browser and reset with `R` between testers.
- Do not explain the geometry. Say only: “Move with WASD. Use Space to surround and capture one creature.”
- All testers start with the shipping default, Toggle.
- After one capture, switch to Hold and let the tester try once more. The comparison validates the fallback; it does not hide default-mode onboarding problems.

## Owner visual sign-off

This check does not count among the five first-time testers. Before recruiting them, confirm that one successful capture clearly shows all four beats: bright closure, organic contraction, enemy decomposition, and reward intake into Carrier-09. Record only what still feels weak; do not add more effects unless one of those beats is unreadable.

## Gate

- 5/5 complete a capture within 30 seconds.
- At least 4/5 rate closure satisfaction 4 or 5.
- At least 4/5 can explain what the anchor and bright snap marker mean.
- No tester believes Space stops body movement or acts like a normal attack.

If the gate fails, change snap radii, minimum area, preview contrast, or input default before adding enemy systems. Do not solve a control failure with more tutorial text first.
