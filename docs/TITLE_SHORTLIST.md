# Title Decision

Status: **FLESHLOOM selected** · 2026-08-11

`NEON SWARM` should be retired before public release. It describes the discarded neon-cell fantasy more than the current parasitic hunter, and the name is already used by a [Google Play game](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=kr.goldfish.neonswarm), an [upcoming Steam title](https://steamdb.info/app/4943600/), and [Lockstep Arcade](https://locksteparcade.com/gettingstarted/).

## Selected title

### 1. FLESHLOOM

- Best match for the full identity: body horror (`Flesh`) plus a creature that weaves a lethal enclosure (`Loom`).
- Short, pronounceable, visually strong as `FLESH / LOOM`, and broad enough to cover later mutations and the city story.
- Preliminary exact-name searches found no obvious same-title Steam or itch.io game. This is not legal or trademark clearance.

## Alternatives

### 2. KNOT//FEED

- Communicates the signature rhythm directly: make the knot, then feed.
- Strong experimental-indie typography, but `Knot` by itself is crowded and the spoken title is slightly less natural.

### 3. DEVOURLINE

- Immediately readable as predation through a drawn line.
- More action-forward and accessible, but less uncanny and distinctive than `FLESHLOOM`.

## Rejected candidate

### GRAFTLINE

The biological/industrial tone fits, but the name is already strongly associated with [vegetable grafting education](https://graftline.com/), an [automated grafting machine](https://www.tta-iso.com/innovations/graftline), and an [orthopedic product line](https://www.richard-wolf.com/en-uk/disciplines/orthopedics/graftline). It would be difficult to own in search.

## Completion rule

The owner selected `FLESHLOOM` on 2026-08-11. Runtime UI, metadata, README/docs, package name, repository folder, and future deployment slugs use `fleshloom`. `NEON SWARM` remains only in this decision history to explain why it was retired.
